## Definition
The problem demands analysis of a material interface (mixing, interface tracking/location,
interface-condition accuracy) as part of validating it — the presence of a multi-material contact
raises a specific analysis requirement beyond the single-material case.

## Params schema
None.

## Notes
A searchable descriptor in the identity signature (§2.1 item 2). Note the distinction from the
binary `has_internal_boundaries` capability flag: the flag is the fork; this treatment merely
*names* the analysis demand for findability (§2.1 item 2, "searchable descriptors, not additional
forks").
